# Resume scoring task

Below are TWO job descriptions. For each one you are given THREE candidate resumes
(versions A, B and C) generated for the SAME candidate against the SAME job description
by three different versions of a resume-tailoring system.

The candidate's real, fixed career history is:
  - Wealthsimple, Senior Software Engineer, Aug 2025 - Present  (a Canadian fintech;
    products he worked on: Book of Record, Financial Activity Model,
    Financial Calculation Platform, Machine Learning Platform, AI Inference Platform)
  - TEEMA, Senior Software Engineer, Nov 2020 - Jul 2025  (a STAFFING / CONSULTING firm -
    he was placed on client engagements across different industries)
  - earlier roles, rendered verbatim and identical in all versions

Only the Wealthsimple and TEEMA sections are tailored per job. Everything else is fixed.

Please score each version 1-10 on EACH of the following, and explain with quotes:

 1. CREDIBILITY - would an interviewer believe this person did this work at THAT employer?
    Flag anything a fintech backend engineer would not plausibly have owned at Wealthsimple.
 2. COVERAGE - how many of the job description's real requirements and named technologies
    are actually evidenced in the experience bullets (not just listed in Skills)?
 3. COMPANY MISALIGNMENT - does the hiring company's business domain or tech stack leak
    into the Wealthsimple section where it does not belong? Does any single company or
    bullet claim two mutually exclusive tools (e.g. two cloud providers, two IaC tools,
    two CI systems)? Consulting-client work at TEEMA is allowed to be domain-specific,
    because those were client engagements.
 4. RECRUITER POV - skim it for 20 seconds. Does it get a callback? Is it specific
    enough to pass a screen without reading as padded or over-tailored?
 5. FIGURE PLAUSIBILITY - are the metrics believable and non-inflated?

Then give an overall ranking of A, B, C for each JD, and say which single version you
would actually send to the employer, and why.

Do not assume the versions are in chronological or quality order.

What this job involves

The EDP Cloud & DevOps team builds and runs the cloud infrastructure, deployment automation, and enterprise architecture standards that every data and platform team at JLL Technologies depends on. We're looking for an experienced Cloud engineer to own end-to-end design of cloud solutions, drive DevOps and CI/CD best practices, and raise the bar on governance and quality across our Azure and AWS environments.

You'll bring 7+ years of hands-on cloud and DevOps experience, a track record of leading multiple large or complex infrastructure projects, and the judgment to set technical direction across scrum teams without needing to be told what "good" looks like.

What you'll get to do

As a Senior Cloud Engineer, you will have ownership over cloud architecture, deployment automation, and platform reliability across the Enterprise Data Platform. Your work directly serves the data engineering, platform, and application teams who build on top of the infrastructure you design, they depend on you for environments that are secure, scalable, and easy to ship into.

Your mission is to make cloud infrastructure and DevOps practice a force multiplier for engineering, not a bottleneck. This is an opportunity to define how JLL Technologies builds, deploys, and governs cloud infrastructure at enterprise scale, and to see that standard adopted across multiple engineering teams.

Key responsibilities

Cloud Architecture & Solution Design

Act as cloud architect for the Data Platform team, designing end-to-end solutions across compute, networking, identity, storage, and messaging, not just deployment pipelines
Drive cloud architecture decisions across Azure and AWS, ensuring solutions are secure, scalable, and cost-optimized
Define enterprise architecture patterns and cloud best practices that scale across multiple domains and teams

Deployment Automation & CI/CD

Architect and automate deployment solutions across cloud services, including containerized workloads, serverless, and data/messaging services
Standardize CI/CD best practices , pipeline design, branching strategy, automated testing, release gating, and rollback
Lead infrastructure-as-code adoption (Terraform, Bicep, ARM, or CloudFormation) across Azure and AWS

Container Orchestration & Platform Engineering

Manage shared Kubernetes cluster architecture, including namespaces, multi-tenancy, RBAC, and workload isolation
Understand cluster networking (CNI, network policies, service mesh) well enough to design for it and troubleshoot it
Evaluate and introduce new tooling that improves automation, deployment speed, and reliability

Governance, Quality & Reliability

Establish observability standards, monitoring, logging, alerting, and tracing, across services and environments
Define and enforce data and infrastructure governance practices so quality and compliance scale with the platform
Lead incident response and post-mortems for infrastructure and deployment issues

Technical Leadership & Mentorship

Mentor engineers on the team in cloud architecture, DevOps practice, and troubleshooting
Recommend and drive adoption of best practices across scrum teams within the domain
Act as a technical escalation point for complex architecture, deployment, and infrastructure issues

Who you are

You thrive at the intersection of technical depth and cross-team influence — equally comfortable designing a disaster-recovery strategy and explaining why it matters to a room of stakeholders. You challenge the status quo when a process or architecture no longer scales, and you'd rather fix the root cause than patch around it. You take ownership of outcomes across team boundaries, and you enjoy raising the technical bar for the engineers around you.
